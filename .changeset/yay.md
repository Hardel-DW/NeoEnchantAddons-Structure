## NE+ Addons - Structure - Minecraft 26.1 and 26.2
The addon now supports Minecraft 26.1 through 26.2 with a single build.

### Update
- Supported pack format range widened to 101 (26.1) - 107 (26.2).
- No data change was needed: the enchantment tag format is unchanged across those versions.
This addon requires NeoEnchant 5.16.0 or later on 26.2.
